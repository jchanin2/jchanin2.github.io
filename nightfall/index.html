Sunrise on Ninth Street

The Syndicate's tower is dark. Their books are ash. Their vault is a very large, very empty room with excellent acoustics.

Down on Ninth Street, the sun comes up on a city with its money back. Ace folds the last blueprint and slides it across the diner table to you.

"Every lock they had," Ace says. "Distribution, negatives, fractions, forgeries — you cracked all of it. The city doesn't know your name, Cipher. But it's spending your work this morning."

Outside, the van is waiting. Muffin lowers the sunglasses one inch, looks at you over the top of them, and nods.

Highest honor there is.
